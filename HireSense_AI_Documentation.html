1. Executive Summary & Core Mission

HireSense AI is an enterprise talent acquisition web platform designed to eliminate the risks of "black-box" recruitment algorithms. Typical ATS scanners make arbitrary decisions without accountability, hallucinate qualifications, or automatically disqualify promising candidates based on keyword formatting flaws.

HireSense AI enforces an Explainable AI (XAI) and Human-in-the-Loop operational model:

  • Verifiable Evidence Citations: Every single percentage and matched qualification is backed by verbatim excerpts extracted directly from the candidate's uploaded resume.
  • No Unilateral Auto-Rejections: The system surfaces insights, missing mandatory requirements, and skill gaps, while keeping recruiters firmly in control of the final shortlisting choices.
  • Zero-Dependency Resilience: Built with embedded In-Memory MongoDB fallback and an offline TF-IDF cosine-vector fallback, allowing full operational execution immediately upon checkout.

4. Explainable AI Matching Engine & Mathematical Model

To maintain complete transparency and auditability, candidate match calculations are governed by a strictly bounded, 4-factor scoring model. Each component is normalized from 0 to 100 before weighting:

Total Score = (Skills Alignment × 0.40) + (Semantic Vector Similarity × 0.30) + (Experience Alignment × 0.20) + (Qualification Alignment × 0.10)
Component Weight Evaluation Criteria Evidence Produced
Skill Alignment 40% Mandatory skills coverage (85% base) + preferred bonus skills (up to 15%) Direct verbatim sentence quotes from projects/experience
Semantic Similarity 30% Vector cosine similarity between resume text & job requirements Cosine similarity distance metric (0.00 – 1.00)
Experience Fit 20% Total verified experience vs. minimum/maximum job thresholds Calculated total years and verified job tenure dates
Qualification Alignment 10% Degree level (Bachelors, Masters, PhD) & major relevance Extracted institution names, degree levels, graduation years

5. 500+ Skill Taxonomy & Regex Boundary Engine

Technical skills contain unique characters and symbols (e.g. C++, C#, .NET, Node.js) that routinely break naive word boundary parsers (\b). HireSense AI implements a specialized lookahead/lookbehind regex compiler:

  • Punctuation Resilience: Correctly detects TypeScript. at the end of a sentence without dropping the match.
  • Symbol Differentiation: Distinguishes C from C++ and C# with 100% precision.
  • Categorization Engine: Organizes extracted skills into 8 domains: Frontend, Backend, Cloud & DevOps, Databases, AI/ML, Mobile, Security, and Leadership.

6. Privacy, Security & GDPR Compliance

Blind Screening Mode

Recruiters can toggle anonymization to mask candidate names, photos, emails, phone numbers, and gender indicators to eliminate unconscious bias during initial screening.

Automated Data Retention

Configure automatic retention timeframes (90, 180, or 365 days) after which historical resume files and extracted personal identifiers are automatically purged.

GDPR Right-to-be-Forgotten

Candidates can be permanently erased from all collections, including applications, resumes, interview notes, and audit logs with a single action.

Enterprise Audit Trail

All sensitive operations (stage movements, manual score overrides, role reassignments, candidate deletions) are logged in an immutable audit ledger.
